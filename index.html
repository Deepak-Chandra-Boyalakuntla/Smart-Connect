Beginner's Wi-Fi Guide
What is Wi-Fi Signal (dBm)?

Signal power is measured in dBm. Because radio waves lose energy across space, values are negative numbers:

  • ≥ -60 dBm Strong 4K/HD stream, swift video calls
  • -60 to -70 dBm Moderate General LMS access, minor lag
  • < -70 dBm Weak Disconnections & packet loss
Golden Rule: Closer to 0 is stronger! -45 dBm is significantly stronger and faster than -82 dBm.
Responsible Network Testing

SmartClass Connect uses strictly passive, ethical testing:

  • Never collects or stores Wi-Fi passwords.
  • Never captures student web traffic or browsing packets.
  • Operates solely on voluntarily recorded coordinates and signal readings.
Planning tool only. Hardware changes must be done by authorized staff.
